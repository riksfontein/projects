Right now you only need the first card: the ch02 ep16 2-minute sample. It is 12 clips of 10 s. Clip 1 starts from the first frame, and every next clip starts from the last frame of the clip before (TurboFlow does that for you). Everything else on this page is for later and is folded away at the bottom.

  1. Save the first frame. On the card: press "Save 4K first frame (JPG)" and confirm. (It is also attached in the Claude chat.)
  2. TurboFlow → Video. Omni Flash · 16:9 · x1 · 720p (360p off) · 10s · Video mode Start.
  3. Start frame = the saved JPG (Choose Start Frame → Upload New).
  4. Continue from last frame: ON. Never "Different for Each".
  5. Settings: folder ch02ep16-omni · Auto-download videos · 1080p Upscale · custom prefix clip, separator _, start number 1.
  6. Prompts: press "Copy all 12 clip prompts" on the card and paste. The counter must say 12 prompts.
  7. Before Run: the Queue row must show continuous. Cost: about 15 credits per clip, 180 credits for the sample.
  8. After download: clip_001.mp4 … clip_012.mp4 in Downloads\ch02ep16-omni. The assemble command (inside the card) lays the approved ducked narration and music mix under them, which gives the 2:00 sample.
