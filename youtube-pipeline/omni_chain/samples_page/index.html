Omni Sample Packs

Eight 2-minute samples for the new chain method: one first frame, then 12 Omni Flash clips of 10 s, each continuing from the last frame of the one before. Claude made each first frame at the highest image quality (4K, 16:9) and picked from the variants. They are shown on each card: open one, save it, and upload it as the Start frame. Then paste the clip prompts into one Omni Flash Video batch with "Continue from last frame" on. When the clips are downloaded, the assemble command on each card lays the approved narration and music mix under them.
